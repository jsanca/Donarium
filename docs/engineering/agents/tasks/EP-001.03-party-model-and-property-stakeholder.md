# EP-001.03 — Party model + `PropertyStakeholder` + Wizard Step 2

- **Plan:** [EP-001 — First Property Experience](../../plans/DONARIUM-PA-EXP-001-first-property-experience-engineering-plan-proposal.md) (APPROVED r3, 2026-09-02)

- **Depends on:** EP-001.02
- **Blocks:** EP-001.04 (attribution), EP-001.08, EP-001.10, EP-001.13
- **Permitted parallelism:** after EP-001.02, in parallel with EP-001.05, EP-001.07, EP-001.12

## Intent and outcome

Introduce a Party model — `UserRef(UserID)` | `OrganizationRef(OrganizationID)` | `ExternalParty(Name, Email)` — and a `PropertyStakeholder(PropertyID, PartyRef, Role, CreatedAt)` relation. Extend `RegisterProperty` to persist declared stakeholders atomically in the same transaction as the Property. Wizard Step 2 exposes the four Stitch relationship options ("I am both Owner & Manager", "I am Manager on behalf of Owner", "I am Owner delegating to another manager", "Acting on behalf of an Organization"). Property Home header uses the resulting attribution.

## Relevant context and requirements

- `Membership` and `OrganizationRole` are **not** extended. The identity schema is untouched.
- Uniqueness: `(PropertyID, PartyRef, Role)` per plan A-12. The same Party may hold both OWNER and MANAGER on the same Property.
- Access rule finalization (see EP-001.02) lands here: a User has access if a `PropertyStakeholder` names them directly OR names an Organization on which they have a `Membership`.
- Complies with plan hygiene **H-2**: new documentation does not cite the contradicted sections of UC-001, BR-05/06/08, or ADR-001.

## Constraints / established direction

- Additive migration `007_property_stakeholders.{up,down}.sql`.
- **ADR-004** authored: `PropertyStakeholder` as per-property relation rather than extending `OrganizationRole`.
- **ADR-005** authored: Party model (`UserRef` | `OrganizationRef` | `ExternalParty`) — external parties are contact-only and confer no Donarium access.

## Expected evidence

- Integration tests exercising each of the four Stitch relationship options and their post-conditions.
- Access-rule tests covering {User-direct stakeholder, User-via-Organization stakeholder, ExternalParty (no access)}.
- ADR-004 and ADR-005 authored, placed under `knowledge/decisions/`, and linked.
- Draft SQL for `007_property_stakeholders` available for EP-001.10 review.
- Report at `docs/engineering/agents/reports/EP-001.03-party-model-and-property-stakeholder.md`.

## Handoff / stop condition

Supplies stakeholder attribution to EP-001.04 (header) and enables EP-001.13 (invitation acceptance creates a `UserRef` stakeholder). Does not implement invitation issuance, payment logging, or Property editing beyond registration.
