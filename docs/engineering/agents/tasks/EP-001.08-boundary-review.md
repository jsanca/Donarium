## Intent and outcome

Independently assess:

1. The Party model (`UserRef` | `OrganizationRef` | `ExternalParty`) and its persistence shape.
2. The decision to add `PropertyStakeholder` as a per-property relation rather than extending `OrganizationRole`.
3. The identity/properties transactional seam (reuse of `TransactionManager` and `DBExecutor`).
4. Invitation semantics against ADR-006, including the four authorization outcomes and the OP-9 decoupling of invitation creation from delivery.
5. The Payment-log boundary against future Payments-domain freedom (ADR-007).
6. Whether EP-001 documentation avoids repeating DON-007R R-002 (plan hygiene **H-2** compliance).
