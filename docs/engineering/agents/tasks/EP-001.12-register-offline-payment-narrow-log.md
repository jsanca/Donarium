# EP-001.12 — Register Offline Payment (narrow log)

- **Plan:** [EP-001 — First Property Experience](../../plans/DONARIUM-PA-EXP-001-first-property-experience-engineering-plan-proposal.md) (APPROVED r3, 2026-09-02)
- **Assigned Role:** `software-engineer` (v0.2.0)
- **Status:** MATERIALIZED — awaiting Role adoption
- **Depends on:** EP-001.02
- **Blocks:** EP-001.04 (payment section), EP-001.08 (via ADR-007), EP-001.09, EP-001.10, EP-001.11
- **Permitted parallelism:** independent of EP-001.03, .05, .07, .13 once EP-001.02 lands

## Intent and outcome

Introduce a **narrow** `Payment` entity — `(PaymentID, PropertyID, Amount, ReceivedAt, Method, Notes, RecordedBy, CreatedAt)` — with migration `008_payments`, endpoints `POST /api/properties/{id}/payments` and `GET /api/properties/{id}/payments`, and a chronological payment-log section on Property Home. Method enum per plan A-10: `{ check, cash, wire, other }`.

## Relevant context and requirements

- **Deliberately omits** billing schedules, arrears computation, ACH, auto-pay, settlement processing, accounting ledger, on-time ratios, receipt PDFs. The intent is to preserve full design freedom for a future Payments domain.
- Access control: the recorder must have stakeholder access to the Property (User-direct or User-via-Organization).
- Complies with plan hygiene **H-3** and **H-4**.

## Constraints / established direction

- Additive migration `008_payments` only. No modification of existing tables.
- **ADR-007** authored: Payment log representation for the Properties experience — records the narrow scope and explicit non-goals to preserve future Payments-domain freedom.

## Expected evidence

- Integration tests exercising the log post-conditions (record appears in list with correct fields; unauthorized recorder → 403; unknown property → 404).
- ADR-007 authored and linked.
- Draft SQL for `008_payments` available for EP-001.10.
- Report at `docs/engineering/agents/reports/EP-001.12-register-offline-payment-narrow-log.md`.

## Handoff / stop condition

Log-only capability is available. Anything ledger-like, billing-like, or auto-pay-related is out of this node.
