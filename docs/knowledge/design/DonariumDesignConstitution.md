Donarium Design Constitution

Product:
Modern property and rental management platform.

Emotional qualities:
Warm, calm, trustworthy, contemporary, human.

Users:
Independent landlords, property managers, companies and tenants.

Visual direction:
Editorial proptech with subtle hospitality influences.
Spacious layouts, strong typography, architectural imagery,
soft surfaces, restrained accents and purposeful motion.

Avoid:
Generic admin dashboards, excessive tables, corporate blue,
neon gradients, heavy glassmorphism, clutter and playful fintech styling.

Desktop manager experience:
Information-rich but visually hierarchical.

Mobile tenant experience:
Simple, reassuring and action-oriented.

Accessibility:
WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, reduced-motion support,
keyboard navigation and large touch targets.
